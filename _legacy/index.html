05 / FAQ

Straight
answers.

What does HashSlash actually do?

We build digital products (websites, Shopify stores, apps, CRMs and automations), market them (Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO, social and content) and help you scale with strategy, CRO and analytics, all under one team.

Can you take over just one part, like ads or the website?

Yes. Many clients start with a single service. Because tech, marketing and growth sit together, expanding later doesn't mean switching agencies.

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify, WordPress, custom React/Next.js builds, Meta, Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot and other major CRMs, plus AI and automation tooling. If you're already on a platform, we usually work with it rather than replace it.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope, and we'll give you a dated plan after the discovery call. We work in weekly sprints, so you see progress from week one.

How do you report on results?

We set up and verify conversion tracking first, then report against the metrics agreed in your audit: leads, sales, cost per acquisition and revenue.

How do we get started?

Book a free strategy call or send a project enquiry below. You'll hear back from a strategist, not a sales script.
